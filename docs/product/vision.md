# Product Vision v0.3

## Definition

Network Compass is a **Personal Network Platform** that visualizes an employee's internal network as a second social memory, helps relationships remain active and discoverable, and reduces network gaps created by tenure, organizational structure, or chance.

Its three product roles are:

- **Social Memory** — remember who is in the network, how the relationship formed, and shared context.
- **Network Navigator** — understand reachable people and natural paths to them.
- **Network Equalizer** — reduce network disadvantage for people entering a new environment, including graduate and experienced hires.

## Core problem

The organization often has the right people, knowledge, and relationships, but individuals cannot see or recall them. This is the **Invisible Network Problem**.

Opportunity gaps can arise from capability gaps **and** network gaps. The product must help capable people convert experience and intent into access without requiring an existing privileged network.

## Core user loop

`See -> Notice -> Remember/Discover -> Understand -> Act -> Connect -> Learn`

The product should create the feeling: **"自分の社内Networkってこうなっていたのか"**, then make a natural human action possible.

## What it is not

It is not primarily staffing software, a sales CRM, an employee-monitoring system, an influence leaderboard, or a social-media feed.
