# Primary Personas v0.1

## Persona A — Existing Employee

Has an established but imperfectly remembered internal network. Needs to remember dormant contacts, understand relationship history, discover reachable 2-hop people, and maintain a network without turning networking into a full-time task.

## Persona B — Graduate New Joiner

Starts with a cohort-heavy network: peers and immediate team are visible, but senior role models, other teams, communities, and expertise are distant. Ramp emphasis: Belong -> Learn/Connect -> Expand -> Activate.

## Persona C — Experienced New Joiner

May bring significant external capability and experience but has a thin internal network. Ramp emphasis starts with accessible internal context and professional-fit connections earlier than for graduate hires, then expands cross-unit reach.

Hire type is an **initial prior**, not a permanent personality label. Recommendations also use networking preferences and actual network state.
