# Product Principles v0.1

## Person first

The default canvas represents people and human relationships. Context nodes must not overwhelm the graph.

## Human relationships, not scoring

Do not expose relationship or network numbers as a game, ranking, or proxy for employee value. Node size must never encode seniority.

## Social memory before enterprise optimization

The first user value is remembering and reaching people. Knowledge sharing, collaboration, staffing, and business outcomes are downstream effects.

## Network equity

Do not make people with existing strong networks even more advantaged by default. New joiners and people entering new environments need deliberate accessible paths into the network.

## Explainability

The system must be able to explain why a relationship state or recommendation exists using known evidence/reason codes.

## Gentle language

Prefer "久しぶりのつながり" to deterioration language. Do not shame users for small, concentrated, or quiet networks.

## User agency

Users can correct relationship interpretation and control declared social/activity context. AI may draft communication later, but the user decides and sends.

## Privacy by architecture

Individual graph visibility is personal. Aggregate organizational analytics, if added, must not become a back door to individual monitoring.
