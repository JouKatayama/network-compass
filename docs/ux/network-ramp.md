# Network Ramp Screen v0.1 — Later Vertical Slice

Visible to eligible new joiners. Shows phase, a small set of people worth knowing, an understandable network-ramp map/insight, and natural actions.

Graduate: early emphasis on belonging, non-cohort role models, community, then broader network.

Experienced: early emphasis on accessible internal context plus relevant expertise/industry/community paths.

Do not display a low Network Health score to a person who joined recently. Tenure/context-adjusted language is mandatory.
