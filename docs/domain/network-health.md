# Network Health Model v0.1

Network Health is the fit between a person's actual network and the depth, reach, diversity, reciprocity, activation, and renewal that works for that person. It is **not network size**.

## Dimensions

- Activation — meaningful relationships are currently alive
- Depth — enough deep relationships for this person's preference/context
- Reach — accessible relevant organizational/community/expertise domains
- Diversity — concentration across relevant dimensions, compared with a personalized target range
- Reciprocity — relationships are not systematically one-way, adjusted for structurally asymmetric roles
- Renewal — appropriate amount of new/reconnected/cross-cluster relationships
- Resilience — hidden analysis of over-dependence on a few relationships

No canonical total score is user-facing. A composite may exist only as an explicitly experimental analysis artifact.

Dormant ties are not automatically unhealthy. Evaluate valuable dormant ties based on history, relevance, and reconnectability.

Well-being must not be inferred from graph data. A future monthly 3-item Network Support Pulse can ask whether the user has people to consult, feels connected, and can access needed people.
